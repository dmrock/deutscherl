title: 'Word order: the verb in position 2 and questions'
summary: In a German statement, the verb always comes second. If something else comes first, the subject goes right after the verb. Yes/no questions start with the verb. Questions with a question word like *wo* start with that word, and the verb comes second.
---

## The verb in position 2

In a German statement, the verb comes second. This is the verb form that changes with the person, like <De>wohne</De> or <De>arbeitet</De>. A position is a part of the sentence, not a single word: <De>mein Bruder</De> or <De>am Wochenende</De> is one part.

## Something else in position 1

Position 1 does not have to be the subject. You can start with a time, a place or an object, often to stress it. The verb stays in position 2, and the subject moves right after it.

English keeps the subject before the verb: "Today I work at home". German doesn't: after <De>heute</De> comes the verb, then <De>ich</De>.

## Two verbs: the second one goes to the end

When a sentence has two verbs, only the one that changes with the person is in position 2. The other one goes to the very end, and everything else sits between them.

**1. Modal verbs.** With <De>möchten</De>, <De>können</De>, <De>müssen</De> and similar verbs, the infinitive (the basic form, like <De>trinken</De>) goes to the end.

## <De>und</De>, <De>aber</De>, <De>oder</De>, <De>denn</De>

These words join two sentences. They don't take a position: after them, the second sentence starts again with position 1, and its verb comes second. <De>denn</De> means "because".

## Yes/no questions: the verb first

A question you can answer with <De>ja</De> or <De>nein</De> starts with the verb. The subject comes right after it.

## Questions with a question word

A question with a question word starts with that word. The verb comes second, then the subject.

- <De>wer</De> (who), <De>was</De> (what), <De>wie</De> (how)
- <De>wo</De> (where), <De>woher</De> (where from), <De>wohin</De> (where to)
- <De>wann</De> (when), <De>warum</De> (why), <De>wie viel</De> (how much)
