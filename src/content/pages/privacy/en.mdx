## Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to Cloudflare's servers: your IP address, the date and time, the page you requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the page and to protect the site from attacks.

## Visitor statistics

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or browser storage and does not create a profile of you. We see only totals: for example how often a page was opened, from which country and with which kind of browser.

## Storage in your browser

The site stores two small things in your browser. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them:

- **Theme**: if you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved (local storage, key `theme`) until you delete it.
- **Exercises**: the exercise items you have already seen in a topic (session storage, keys `seen:…`), so the next round shows you other items. This is deleted when you close the tab.

You can delete both at any time in your browser's settings.

## Search

The search runs entirely in your browser: it loads the search index from this site, and your search terms are not sent anywhere.

## Links to GitHub

"Report a mistake" and the other GitHub links take you to GitHub (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub's privacy statement applies there. We only see what you write in an issue.
