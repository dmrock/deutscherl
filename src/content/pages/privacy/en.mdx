## Hosting

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site and processes data on our behalf. To deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, the page you open and your browser. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare may process this data in the USA; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Statistics

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. On each page, a script from Cloudflare measures the page view and how fast the page loads and sends this to Cloudflare. It uses no cookies, and Cloudflare states that it does not track individual visitors. We only see totals, for example how often a page was opened, from which country, and with which browser and type of device.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read, so we can improve the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Storage period:** Cloudflare keeps the complete data for 7 days. After that it keeps a reduced sample of about 10% of the data, and we can view the statistics for the last six months.

## Your browser

The site stores two things in your browser:

- **Theme:** if you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in local storage under the key `theme` until you delete this site's data in your browser.
- **Seen exercises:** which exercises of a topic you have already seen, so that "Try again" shows new ones. They are saved in session storage under a key such as `seen:a2/perfekt` and deleted when you close the tab.

This data never leaves your device, and we never see it. Both are strictly necessary for functions you use yourself (the theme switch and the exercises), so they need no consent (§ 165(3) <De>TKG 2021</De>).
