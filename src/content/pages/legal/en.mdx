## Privacy

This site has no accounts or forms, does not use cookies and shows no ads with trackers. The person named above is responsible for the data processing described here.

- **Hosting:** the site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. To deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, the page you open and your browser. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare may process this data in the USA; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Statistics:** we count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies; we only see totals, for example how often a page was opened and from which country.
- **Your browser:** the site saves your theme (if you switch it) and the exercises you have already seen in a topic (until you close the tab). This stays on your device; we never see it.
- **Search** runs in your browser; your search terms are not sent anywhere.
- **GitHub:** "Report a mistake" and the other GitHub links take you to GitHub, where GitHub's privacy statement applies.
