This is a free website for learning German grammar. Every topic has a short explanation in your language, examples in German with a translation, and exercises with an explanation for each answer. You do not need an account, and the site does not use cookies.

Notes marked "In Austria" show where people in Austria say something differently from Germany.

## How the topics are checked

The person who runs the site is learning German too, so every topic goes through checks before you can rely on it. The badges at the top of each topic say what has been checked:

- **Draft**: the topic is new and nobody has checked it yet.
- **Checked by a teacher**: a German teacher checked the German examples, the exercises and the English explanation.
- **Translation reviewed**: a fluent speaker checked that the translation says the same as the English page.

When a checked text changes, its badge goes back to **Draft** until it is checked again.

We write every topic ourselves and check it against grammar references.

## Found a mistake?

After each exercise answer there is a small flag icon: it opens a short form on GitHub with the page and the exercise already filled in. You can also [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/dmrock/deutscherl/issues/new/choose) directly. You need a free GitHub account for both.

## Accessibility

We aim for [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) level AA on every page. In particular:

- Everything works with a keyboard, and the pages are built for screen readers (headings, labels, announcements in the exercises). German text is marked as German, so screen readers read it with German pronunciation.
- In word-order exercises you do not have to drag: a tap or a click (or Enter on the keyboard) moves a word into the sentence or back. Dragging is only an extra way to change the order.
- The site has a light and a dark theme and follows your system setting.
- The site does not use time limits, sound or moving content you cannot stop.
