## Prepositions with one case

<GermanTable id="prepositions">Prepositions with one case</GermanTable>

<De>bis</De> usually comes before a word without an article: <De>bis Montag</De>, <De>bis Wien</De>. When a second preposition follows it, that preposition decides the case.

In speech, you also hear <De>wegen</De> with the dative: <De>wegen dem Wetter</De>. In writing, use the genitive.
